Your data

What SPY records, and what it never does.

SPY records

  • The name of the app in front and its window title
  • Start and end times, and idle time
  • The website name in supported browsers, only if you turn that setting on (never the page path or search text)

SPY never records

  • Keystrokes or what you type
  • Screenshots
  • Microphone or camera data
  • File contents

Everything stays in a folder on your computer and is not sent to us. One optional setting, off by default, looks up YouTube channel names through YouTube's public service.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Does SPY send my data anywhere?

No. Everything SPY records stays on your computer. The only optional internet request is the YouTube channel lookup, which is off by default.

Is SPY spyware?

No. SPY is made for seeing your own computer use. It is not meant to be installed on someone else's computer without their knowledge, and it is not marketed for secret monitoring.

What happens after the 3-day trial?

Tracking pauses until you enter a license key. Your records are kept and can be exported.

Can I export or delete my data?

Yes. Export to CSV or JSON, clear your history at any time, or set automatic deletion of older records.

Which languages does SPY support?

Turkish, English, German, Spanish, French, Portuguese, Russian, Italian and Ukrainian.

How do I get support?
