## Full description (max 4000 characters)
```
See where your phone time goes and win your focus back.

Spy records how long you spend in each app and shows it as a clear picture: how much is productive, how much is entertainment or distraction? It is the phone edition of Spy for computers, with the same categories, the same focus score and the same Spy eye.

WHAT'S INSIDE

• Overview: today's total time, category breakdown, your most used apps and your daily goal.
• Analysis: today, the last 7 days and the last 30 days; usage shares, trend, your busiest day and the change versus the previous period.
• Timeline: see which app you were in at each hour of the day as a strip.
• Focus session: start an uninterrupted work session; Spy counts the app switches that pull you away and gives a live focus score.
• Pomodoro: work and break phases. While a session runs, the time left shows in the notification bar, where you can pause or end it. You get notified when a phase ends.
• Chat with Spy: answers questions like "How did I use my phone today?", "What distracts me the most?" and "What is my most productive hour?", and offers small tips.
• Manage: change an app's category yourself; the change applies to past days too.
• Spy eye: 10 moods that change with the situation, animations and short sounds. Turn them off in Settings if you like.
• Export and backup: save your records as CSV and back up your settings.

PRIVACY COMES FIRST

• Your data stays on your phone. The app has no internet permission, so nothing can be sent anywhere.
• No account, no ads, no analytics tools, no third-party trackers.
• Your messages, screen content, location and contacts are never recorded. Only the app name and the time used are kept.
• Exclude any app from recording, have old records deleted automatically, or erase everything with one tap.

IMPORTANT

Spy only shows usage on your own phone. It does not run in secret and is not designed to monitor anyone else. You grant Android's "Usage access" permission yourself and can take it back in Settings at any time.

PRICE

Try Spy free for 3 days. To continue, a one-time "Spy Pro" purchase is all it takes: no subscription, no hidden fees. When the trial ends your records are not deleted; only new recording stops.

Questions and suggestions: spyisletme@gmail.com
```
